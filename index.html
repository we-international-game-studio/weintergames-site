Two picture puzzles built on the same idea: a challenge that is truly hard, and a painting worth finishing.

In review for Google Play

Swapscape

Every picture has been cut into cards and shuffled. Swap them back. Cards that belong together stick, so you can move whole pieces at once. But moves are limited, some cards are turned around, and the toughest levels run against the clock.

  • 48 hand-picked scenes in 6 chapters
  • Hard, Very hard and Very very hard. No easy mode.
  • Collect every finished picture in your album
  • No ads. Plays offline. English and Thai.

Closed testing on Google Play

Blockscape

Drop wooden blocks on the board. Fill a row or a column and it clears. Clear lines through the gold frames and a hidden painting peeks through, piece by piece.

  • 20 handcrafted levels, endless Classic and a Daily puzzle
  • A weekly Gallery of five special levels
  • Fair pieces: every tray has a piece that fits
  • No ads while you play. Plays offline. English and Thai.
