Privacy policy

Brickbloom privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. Brickbloom: Brick Breaker Art is made by WE International Game Studio, operated by We International Education & Visa Pty Ltd, Sydney, Australia.

Englishภาษาไทย

What we collect

Nothing. Brickbloom does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins and settings) is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads and analytics

This version has no advertising and no analytics. If a later version adds them, we will update this policy before that version is released and say what is collected and why.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and change the date above.
