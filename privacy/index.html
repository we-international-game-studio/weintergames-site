Privacy

Privacy at WE International Game Studio

Updated 13 November 2026

Our games are built to work without knowing who you are. They need no account, collect no personal data and save your progress only on your device. Each game has its own policy:

  • Swapscape: Picture Swap
  • Blockscape: Block Puzzle Art

This website

weintergames.com sets no cookies and runs no tracking or analytics. Like every website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Emails you send us

If you email us for support or a playtest invite, we use your email address only to reply and, for playtests, to add you to the test list in Google Play. We never sell it or share it for marketing. Ask us any time and we will delete it.
