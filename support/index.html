Common questions

My progress is gone.
Progress is saved only on your phone. If the app was deleted or the phone was reset, it cannot be restored, because we never receive a copy.
Something looks broken.
Email games@weintervisa.com with the game name, your phone model and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
Can I play on iPhone?
Not yet. Our games start on Google Play; an App Store version of Swapscape is being prepared.
How do I join a playtest?
Email us the Google account you use on your Android phone and we will send you an invite.
